COMPONENT HIERARCHY DIAGRAM
============================

App  (manages: user, activePage, pageParams)
|
+-- NotifyToast  [always mounted, listens to NotifyService]
|
+-- [When NOT logged in]
|   +-- LoginPage
|   +-- RegisterPage
|
+-- [When logged in - full layout]
    +-- NavBar  (props: user, onLogout)
    |
    +-- SideMenu  (props: role, activePage, onNavigate)
    |   +-- [Teacher items]: My Exams | Create Exam | Student Results
    |   +-- [Student items]: Available Exams | My Results
    |
    +-- Main Content  (route rendered by activePage state)
        |
        +-- [Teacher Routes]
        |   +-- ExamList          (my-exams)
        |   +-- ExamForm          (create-exam)   -- no examId prop
        |   +-- ExamForm          (edit-exam)     -- with examId prop
        |   +-- StudentResults    (student-results)
        |
        +-- [Student Routes]
        |   +-- AvailableExams    (available-exams)
        |   +-- TakeExam          (take-exam)     -- with examId prop
        |   +-- MyResults         (my-results)
        |
        +-- ComingSoon            (fallback placeholder)


SHARED COMPONENTS
-----------------
components/shared/
  NotifyToast.jsx    - Toast notification renderer (subscribes to NotifyService)
  ComingSoon.jsx     - Placeholder for unimplemented pages


NAVIGATION COMPONENTS
---------------------
components/navigation/
  NavBar.jsx         - Top bar: app name, user info, logout button
  SideMenu.jsx       - Left sidebar: role-based menu items, active highlight


TEACHER COMPONENTS
------------------
components/teacher/
  ExamList.jsx       - Exam cards with status, actions, submission counts
  ExamForm.jsx       - Create/Edit exam form with dynamic question builder
  StudentResults.jsx - Per-exam results table with stats


STUDENT COMPONENTS
------------------
components/student/
  AvailableExams.jsx - Published exams list, attempt status check
  TakeExam.jsx       - Exam runner: timer, questions, submit, result review
  MyResults.jsx      - Student's attempt history with stats
